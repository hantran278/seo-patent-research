SUMMARY:  ## SUMMARY Disclosed herein are apparatus, methods and systems for the detection and processing of malware in sponsored content. In an implementation, intrusion features of a landing page associated with sponsored content are identified. A feature score for the landing page based on the identified intrusion features is generated, and if the feature score for the landing page exceeds a feature threshold, the landing page is classified as a candidate landing page.
